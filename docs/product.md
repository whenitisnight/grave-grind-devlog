# Product brief

Grave / Grind combines arcade skating with survival action. The player should be able to keep moving, chain tricks and use the city while dealing with enemies and building a run.

## Intended experience

- Momentum and responsive trick inputs make movement satisfying in its own right.
- Rails, ledges, ramps and buildings form connected routes through the city.
- Survival pressure gives those routes a purpose during a run.
- Camera and animation keep the board, landing opportunities and nearby threats readable.

The references are Tony Hawk's Pro Skater for skating and Megabonk for survival gameplay. Exact equivalence to either game is an unverified goal, not a completed feature.

## Current implementation focus

The prototype has skating, tricks, surface interactions, camera and rider presentation, plus combat, enemies, pickups and run-progression systems. Recent verification focused on movement. Having these systems implemented does not establish that their combination has reached the intended quality.

The latest street-line work resolves specific rail-acquisition, edge-contact and shelter-camera defects. The next broader focus is how the skating foundation behaves within survival gameplay, including enemy pressure and visual readability.

## Scope and status

Unreal Engine 5.8; desktop prototype first. Mobile adaptation, music and detailed balancing are deferred. Additional level themes are concepts until implemented and demonstrated.

The development journal records both improvements and remaining limitations. It uses AI-assisted development and distinguishes automated checks from hands-on play.
